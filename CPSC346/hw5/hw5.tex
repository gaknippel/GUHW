\section*{a)}

the number of virtual pages is the size of the virtual address space divided by the page size:
\[
\#\text{virtual pages} = \frac{2^{\text{virtual bits}}}{\text{page size}}
= \frac{2^{\text{virtual bits}}}{2^{\text{offset bits}}}
= 2^{\,\text{virtual bits} - \text{offset bits}}
\]

the offset needs $\log_2(\text{page size})$ bits, and the page number uses the remaining bits.
so the maximum number of virtual pages is $2^{p}$

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\toprule
 & design 1 & design 2 & design 3 \\
\midrule
virtual address bits & 16 & 32 & 36 \\
page size & $32 = 2^5$ & $64 = 2^6$ & $128 = 2^7$ \\
offset bits $|d|$ & 5 & 6 & 7 \\
page number bits $|p|$ & $16-5=11$ & $32-6=26$ & $36-7=29$ \\
\midrule
\textbf{max virtual pages} & $\mathbf{2^{11}}$ & $\mathbf{2^{26}}$ & $\mathbf{2^{29}}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{design 1:} $\dfrac{2^{16}}{2^{5}} = 2^{11} = \mathbf{2{,}048}$ pages
  \item \textbf{design 2:} $\dfrac{2^{32}}{2^{6}} = 2^{26} = \mathbf{67{,}108{,}864}$ pages
  \item \textbf{design 3:} $\dfrac{2^{36}}{2^{7}} = 2^{29} = \mathbf{536{,}870{,}912}$ pages
\end{itemize}


\section*{b)}

the number of physical pages is the size of physical memory divided by the page size:
\[
\#\text{physical pages} = \frac{2^{\text{physical bits}}}{\text{page size}}
= 2^{\,\text{physical bits} - \text{offset bits}}
\]

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{design 1:} $\dfrac{2^{10}}{2^{5}} = 2^{5} = \mathbf{32}$ pages
  \item \textbf{design 2:} $\dfrac{2^{20}}{2^{6}} = 2^{14} = \mathbf{16{,}384}$ pages
  \item \textbf{design 3:} $\dfrac{2^{24}}{2^{7}} = 2^{17} = \mathbf{131{,}072}$ pages
\end{itemize}

\section*{c)}

splits into outer, inner, and offset:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{outer:} 128 entries $= 2^{7}$, so $\mathbf{7}$ bits
  \item \textbf{offset:} 128-byte page $= 2^{7}$, so $\mathbf{7}$ bits
  \item \textbf{inner:} the rest, $36 - 7 - 7 = \mathbf{22}$ bits
\end{itemize}
